About

I work at the intersection of artificial intelligence and national security, helping government and defence organizations put real governance around AI systems — so they can adopt the technology at scale without absorbing risk they can't see or manage.

Most recently I was Senior Advisor and AI Governance Lead at Canada's Defence AI Centre, serving as lead governance architect for the framework supporting the DND/CAF AI Strategy. Before that I spent three years at Global Affairs Canada as Senior Advisor for AI and Emerging Technologies, leading departmental engagement on AUKUS Pillar 2 and advising senior officials on frontier AI, semiconductor supply chains, and global AI governance. Earlier postings took me to Kandahar as a political officer, to embassy threat assessment across Sub-Saharan Africa, and to the Afghanistan Task Force.

I'm not only a policy person. I hold an M.P.S. from NYU's Interactive Telecommunications Program, taught creative coding at American University, spent years building interactive installations as a freelance technologist, and in 2025 built and trained a transformer language model from scratch. Hands-on with the technology, fluent in the institutions — that combination is what I bring to a governance problem.
