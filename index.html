The Problem

Law firms are under pressure to adopt AI. Clients now expect faster turnaround. Opposing counsel is using AI to prepare.

A firm must adopt or create tools that can:

  • Cut down on time manually reviewing confidential client documents — identify key issues of a case and surface the applicable documents
  • Query external case law to find relevant precedent
  • Automate the client intake process and prepare response documents

Added to all this, a law firm must be sure that any AI system it uses doesn't break solicitor-client privilege and does not surface confidential information in an LLM system that would compromise the firm's reputation.

Reputational risks are real. So are the time-saving benefits. The question is which tools are worth your time, how to use them without creating professional liability, and how to get your team actually using them.
